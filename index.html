About

Healing is supported through connection. I provide a kind, calm, and compassionate presence that is client-centred and grounded in a strong therapeutic alliance.

We will work together at your own pace to develop an actionable plan based on your values and goals.

I have a special interest in supporting individuals through significant life transitions including grief, infertility, and relationship challenges.

My approach integrates cognitive, emotional, and somatic processing grounded in evidence-based practice and human connection.
